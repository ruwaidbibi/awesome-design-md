The shop

Barbers people follow

Ask for your barber

Customers here talk about their barber by name and keep going back to them for years. Several of the chairs have their own regulars. Ask who is free, or ask for the one you saw last time.

What to expect in the chair

Bring a reference photo if you have one. Expect to be asked what you actually want, and to get an opinion on what will grow out well. The room is modern and clean, and the people in it are easy to be around.

Kids welcome

Plenty of people bring their sons and nephews here alongside their own appointment.
